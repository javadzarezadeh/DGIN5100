Syllabus

This course provides an introduction to
the principles and practices of digital game design. Students will explore game mechanics, level design, storytelling, user experience, and prototyping. The course will also cover the use of game engines and development tools, as well as art, animation, sound design, and monetization strategies. By the end of the course, students will have developed a final project that demonstrates their understanding of digital game design concepts.

Assignments

Throughout the course, students will complete various assignments that will help them apply the concepts learned in class. Assignments will include game design documents, prototypes, playtesting reports, and a final project presentation.

  1. Introduction to Digital Game Design
  2. Game Mechanics and Dynamics
  3. Level Design and Storytelling
  4. User Experience and Interface Design
  5. Prototyping and Playtesting
  • Game Engines and Development Tools
  • Art and Animation in Games
  • Sound Design and Music
  • Monetization Strategies
  • Final Project and Presentation
